## 📄 Featured Research Paper

**Title:** *Graph Theory Risk Propagation in Stochastic environments: An approach into pipeline industrial PdM IoT based modelling and financial contagion risk modelling*  
**Название:** *Распространение рисков на основе теории графов в стохастических средах: подход к моделированию промышленных трубопроводов (IoT/PdM) и финансового заражения*  
**Author / Докладчик:** **Mateus H. N. Lofrano (Матеус Х. Н. Лофрано)**  
**Advisor / Научный руководитель:** **Prof. Gyula O. H. Katona (проф. Дьюла О. Х. Катона)** (Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics / MIPT)  
**Affiliation:** Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT)  
**Digital Archive:** MIPT Digital Repository [https://lib.mipt.ru/](https://lib.mipt.ru/)  

### Key Theoretical Contributions
1. **Mathematical Demarcation:** Formally separates *conservative physical circulation networks* (governed by Kirchhoff laws and Totally Unimodular incidence matrices; van Lint & Wilson 2001) from *non-conservative credit lattices* (governed by Eisenberg-Noe Tarski fixed-point operators and DebtRank single-burn equity destruction; Battiston et al. 2012).
2. **Directed Cheeger Inversion Correction:** Replaces the symmetric Laplacian with Chung's normalized directed Laplacian $\mathcal{L}$, proving that higher directed spectral gap $\lambda_2$ implies ultra-fast mixing of distress, while lower $\lambda_2$ represents Cheeger bottleneck trapping.
3. **Dependent Percolation via Lovász Local Lemma:** Demonstrates why standard independent bond percolation underestimates tail risk in financial runs (e.g., SVB 2023) and multi-point pipeline ruptures, establishing formal dependency graph bounds via the symmetric/asymmetric Lovász Local Lemma (Tao & Vu 2006).
